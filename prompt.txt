Build the best cooking game you can for the make.fun bounty
"best-cooking-game-20261125-0137": a complete, original, playable kitchen
game in plain static files under game/ (no build step, no network request).

Game: "Sizzle & Serve". The lunch rush is on: order tickets arrive on the
order board with a countdown, and the player builds each dish on the plate
from eight ingredient bins on the counter. Patties must be grilled (4s) and
dough baked in the oven (5s) before they can go on the plate. Serve the
exact dish an order asks for (Space or the SERVE button) to score points,
coins and a combo bonus; a wrong dish or an expired order costs one of
three lives. A 2-minute shift, then the game-over screen with the score,
dishes served and best combo, and a restart.

Controls: mouse + keyboard (keys 1-8 pick ingredients, Space serves,
T trashes the plate, M mutes, Enter starts, R restarts). Sounds and a
cheerful music loop are synthesized live with WebAudio — no audio files.

Screenshots: the game supports ?shot=start|play|end&seed=N, which stages
deterministic states (title, mid-shift with a dish half-built on the plate,
game over) so its maker can capture real renders of the game's own code.

Entry: game/ (index.html + src/*.js, no libraries), screenshots/ (3 JPEGs),
main.ts hands game/ and screenshots/ to /output with a dossier built by
vendor/makefun-game.ts. Original idea, art, sounds, music and names.
